**Validation window clarification (fixes the timing gap in Scenario A/I):**

"≥3 buyers purchase a second report within the validation window" is measured over **60–90 days after each buyer's first report is delivered** — not just during the outreach period for the first 20 prospects. Practical effect on the tracker: keep the sheet open and keep checking the "Actually bought second audit" column for each paid customer through their individual 60–90 day window, even after outreach to the initial 20 has finished.
